# Phase3 hotel authoring handoff

Four authored meshes, eight built-in generated concepts and their full prompts, institutional research, independent UV layouts, exact-dimensional GLB inputs, per-asset metadata, and rebuild/review scripts are provided in this folder and `work/hub_hotels`. Root owns Rodin, baking, final FBX exports, manifest and final textured reviews. No Rodin/key access was performed here.

All meshes are in metres, +Z up, front -Y, footprint-centre-ground origin, rotation0/scale1, one mesh and one `M_<id>` material. Glass is opaque prep colour. Lettering is separate: `SIGN_Roosevelt` attaches to the25m rooftop backing; `SIGN_HollywoodHotel` attaches to the main central gable. Metadata gives exact attachment sizes/centres. The Roosevelt main mass is74x58x24; sign/supports reach27.1m, permitted roof dressing. Main HollywoodHotel includes a2cm front projection for its sign backing. The measured float tolerance on116m extents is0.000004m.

The Roosevelt retains regular square windows, upper grouped arches, cream stucco and terracotta pent-roof edges from the historic architecture, compressed to the required seven floors and rectangular collision box. Its actual historic asymmetrical T plan is not reproduced. HollywoodHotel uses three readable levels, long verandas, round columns, dark timber rails, green/cream striped awnings, Mission gables and hipped slate-brown roofs. Main rear and both long wing faces are fully finished for courtyard visibility. The supplied assembly produces the required16m north entrance gap. Historic research images are visual research only, never runtime texture sources.

Triangle counts: Roosevelt9656/20000, HollywoodHotel8743/10000, Wing4300/10000, CourtWall732/10000. The complete HollywoodHotel assembly is18807 triangles (main+2 wings+2 courtyard walls); Roosevelt adds9656. Strict checks import the original GLB, weld1e-6, triangulate and report zero branch edges, loose vertices, zero-area faces, orientation discontinuities and UV-zero-area triangles. The actual `asset_io` FBX export/import roundtrip also passes those checks. Results, bounds, counts and immutable input SHA256 hashes are recorded in `strict_validation.json`. Ground/skin boundaries remain open because hidden undersides and wall thickness are omitted; these are facade surfaces, not watertight collision models. Root should repeat final textured FBX validation before publication.

UVs use padded disjoint feature regions at2K, with congruent bay repeats. The upper triple arches have a dedicated region distinct from square windows. Terracotta coping/ridges have a region distinct from slate/tar roofs and cream trim. Canvas stripes and valances use continuous coordinates. Typical street bay/door texel density is67px/m; main gable54.6px/m; roof patches78px/m and tile strips35.8px/m. Small metal53.8px/m; roof sign backing30.7px/m with lettering on its separate sign card. Return-to-authored-UV baking should choose a representative repeating feature for each region and then broadcast its texture across congruent instances, avoiding conflicting selected-to-active writes from every repeated orientation.

Rebuild geometry: Blender5.2 `--background --python src/hub_hotels/build.py`; optionally list asset IDs after `--`. Render geometry review: Blender `--background --python src/hub_hotels/render.py`, then Python `src/hub_hotels/contact.py`. The geometry-only workbench contact sheet is a preparation inspection, not final textured delivery. Root must inspect textured front/back/roof views.
