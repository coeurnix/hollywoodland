Known modelling interpretation: ground-facing surfaces, wall-rear interior faces and column caps at pavement/lintel contact are removed, so intended boundary edges exist. Tiny brace/pot/trunk/timber joinery overlaps remain; visible surfaces are preserved. Roof shapes and major wall faces are finished for rear and overhead views. The historical forms are deliberately compressed/widened to SPEC collision boxes; these are not measured historical replicas. No real reference photos/postcards were copied into textures.
