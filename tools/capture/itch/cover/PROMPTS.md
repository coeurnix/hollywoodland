## Pass 1: scene

References: `source-picture-night-later.png` and `source-hive-surge.png`, extracted from the existing in-engine capture GIFs in the parent directory. Output: `01-scene-master.png`.

```text
Use case: ads-marketing, screenshot-based gaming creator thumbnail.
Edit the supplied actual Hollywoodland gameplay screenshot into a compelling clean cover composition. This is PASS ONE: scene only, no added headline yet.
Target aspect 630:500 (1.26:1 landscape, a little wider than square); ideally generate 1260x1000 with safe space for later export to exactly630x500.
Retain the actual game's stylized low-poly 3D rendering, its recognizable 1930 Hollywood Boulevard architecture, Chinese theatre facade, ornate street lamps, broad tiled sidewalks, street and groups of varied period-clothed player avatars. This must clearly look like THIS GAME captured at an exciting moment, not a glossy painted film poster or new AAA world.
Remove the screenshot HUD: health panels, minimap, quest tracker, action bars, chat box, floating name labels and speech bubbles. Reframe the existing scene to make the crowd much more legible in a compact almost-square composition, with the boulevard receding diagonally and theatre architecture visible. Bring the existing varied players into a naturally gathered foreground/midground, like a lively social hub. A few larger avatars with readable faces/poses near the bottom, the rest gathered on the pavement. Keep proportions, clothes, render style and buildings faithful to the capture; no photoreal humans or elaborate new landmarks. Mild contrast and exposure improvement, warm late-afternoon golden light, clean color separation between people and background. Do not add monsters, weapons, explosions, arrows, reaction faces, text or watermarks.
Leave the upper quarter relatively simple/darker architecture or sky so large title lettering can be added there in pass two. Keep important players visible in the middle and lower half. The screenshot and a visibly social world are the story.
```
